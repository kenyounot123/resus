# Study

## Sub-features

Recall approved cards and rate memory offline. Practice accepts a written answer and compares it with the approved answer.

## How to get to it (user POV)

Study today in the sidebar opens due cards. Cards tab opens all current approved cards. Practice in the note header opens written practice.

## Driving it with cua_repl

Before Reveal answer, confirm no answer text is visible. Reveal, open Source, and rate Remembered or Review again. Complete a session. Open Practice, type Your answer, and Compare answer. Inspect persisted due dates and review counts.

## Gotchas

Practice is self-rated. There is no AI grader. Changed-source cards are blocked. Source consulting before reveal is assisted recall.
